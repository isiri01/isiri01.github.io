Isiri Withanawasam

I am particularly interested in computer vision and multimodal large language models (MLLMs), especially in how these models learn visual representations and reason across different modalities. I am also interested in exploring how concepts from computational neuroscience and biological learning can be integrated into computer vision and MLLMs to build AI systems that learn and reason in more human-like ways.

I graduated from the University of Moratuwa, Sri Lanka, with a B.Sc. in Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering. My undergraduate thesis, PathKGen: Pathology Knowledge Assisted MLLM for Explainable Histopathology, was conducted with Dr. Ranga Rodrigo and Hasindri Watawana. I developed a vision-language system for whole-slide histopathology analysis by combining visual representations, pathology-specific text, and a curated knowledge graph, with the aim of generating explainable diagnostic reports. The work is currently under review at WACV 2027.

During my undergraduate studies, I worked as a Research Intern at the A*STAR Institute of High Performance Computing, Singapore, where I investigated data heterogeneity in clustered federated learning and developed multi-metric approaches for client clustering. I am currently working as an AI Engineer at Pekoe, contributing to the improvement of a Transformer-based tea auction price prediction model for production.

During my free time I love to watch movies and do hiking to explore the nature.
